# stages/evaluate

Evaluation, per-episode selection, packing and board checks. The submitted rollout is the 1-world export of the official evaluator (evaluator package 8c778f9). The expectation for selection comes from the
4096-world evaluation against the reference of the same tier. The three tiers have separate scores, and you cannot compare scores between tiers. For the method, see
[Sections 4.5–4.7 of the engineering handbook](../../docs/engineering/v2d-track2-engineering-handbook.zh.md) (in Chinese). For the contract, see [`docs/contracts/evaluate.md`](../../docs/contracts/evaluate.md).

Entry points:

- `export_chain.sh`: the export chain. For each checkpoint, it does a 1-world export and a 4096-world evaluation.
- `select_t1.py`, `select_t2.py`: the per-episode selection rules for Tier 1 and Tier 2.
- `make_frame_refs_t3.py`, `align_frefs_t3.py`: the Tier 3 frame references and the frame-0 alignment (the only place that reads Tier 1 frame 0).
- `pack_tier.sh`: calls the official packer to pack, and runs a `--dry-run` precheck. It does not upload.
- `board_diff.py`: a line-by-line comparison of two leaderboard snapshots.

Most other files are one-time diagnostic and analysis scripts. Their conclusions are in the applicable plans in `agent-doc/`. Their output is in `results/`.
